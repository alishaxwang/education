Ferris Wheel Height Graph

Click through one vocabulary word at a time. The rider starts at the lowest point, so the model uses negative cosine.

Calculations

Maximum
Maximum = 50 ft
This is the highest point on the graph.
Minimum
Minimum = 10 ft
This is the lowest point on the graph.
Midline
(50 + 10) / 2
= 30 ft
Halfway between maximum and minimum.
Amplitude
(50 - 10) / 2
= 20 ft
Distance from the midline to the top or bottom.
Period
Period = 12 sec
One full cycle goes from t = 0 to t = 12.
Model
h(t) = 30 - 20cos(2πt / 12)
Uses negative cosine because the rider starts at the minimum height.
