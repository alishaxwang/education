Use this for direct instruction: define maximum, minimum, midline, amplitude, and period using the color-coded Ferris wheel and graph. The orange cart and black graph point show the same moment in time.

Exact Calculations From the Graph

1. Maximum & Minimum
Maximum = 50 ft
Minimum = 10 ft
Read the highest and lowest points on the height graph.
2. Midline
(50 + 10) / 2
= 60 / 2
= 30 ft
The midline is halfway between maximum and minimum.
3. Amplitude
(50 - 10) / 2
= 40 / 2
= 20 ft
Amplitude is the distance from the midline to the top or bottom.
4. Period & Model
Period = 12 sec
h(t) = 30 + 20sin(2πt / 12)
One full cycle is from t = 0 to t = 12 seconds.
